Observation, with provenance.

BTC, ETH, market capitalization, and dominance use public CoinGecko snapshots. Funding and open interest use Binance BTCUSDT perpetual endpoints.

Volatility, when available, is calculated from daily BTC log returns over the last 30 days and annualized using √365.

Public endpoints can be delayed, rate-limited, or unavailable. Stablecoin flows and macro are conceptual lenses without a connected live source. No substitute numbers are generated.

Paths, relationships, and highlights are illustrative. They are not market predictions or trading recommendations.
